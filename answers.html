How to ask

  1. List every surface: siding, roof, driveway, deck.
  2. Ask which method and PSI or soft-wash chemical for each surface.
  3. Get the quote with separate line items before booking.

FAQ

Will high pressure damage my vinyl siding?

Yes. High pressure can etch or force water behind vinyl. Soft wash uses low pressure plus detergent for siding and roofs.

Does the driveway need a different method?

Concrete usually needs higher pressure or a surface cleaner after soft-wash pretreatment. Confirm the line items.
