Soft Or Blast

Charlotte house and roof soft wash vs concrete high-pressure blast

The decision

Charlotte humidity grows algae on siding and roofs. High pressure etches vinyl and lifts shingles. Concrete needs more force. Ask for soft wash on the house and roof, blast on the driveway.
